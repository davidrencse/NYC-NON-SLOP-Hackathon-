## Keep / cut intentionally

Keep explicit no-solution, curated provenance, original system warnings, optional evidence details, labeled offline mode, procurement no-match copy (`App.tsx:474`) and revision rejection. These are strengths, not polish to remove.

Remove from the consumer path: agent leaderboard/token counts, provider setup, authorship-as-truth indicators, CAD format parade, automatic purchase/apply invitations, generic motivational filler. Do not delete engineering capabilities globally. Standard cards and animations are not inherently slop; no visual/browser evidence establishes that they are excessive here. Prefer a compact comparison table because this task compares the same constraints across three motors.

No observed basis to accuse this baseline of “democratization,” “revolution,” fabricated confidence percentages or sentimental personalization. Those are prohibited future copy patterns, not invented findings. Longer advisory explanations should become evidence-linked bullets, not chain-of-thought. No arbitrary score assigned.
